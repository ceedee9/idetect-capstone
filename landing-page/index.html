About iDETECT

Trusted validation, with recourse

iDETECT is an OCR-based discount ID validation system for food service establishments in Valenzuela City. It helps staff check Senior Citizen and PWD IDs against a registry. It helps OSCA, PDAO and BPLO review suspicious cases. And it gives beneficiaries a way to see how their ID is used and to contest mistakes.

The problem we address

Fake Senior Citizen and PWD IDs are a reported national concern. Many portals check only the ID number that staff type in. Nothing reports back to the offices that issue the IDs, and legitimate beneficiaries have no way to see or dispute what happened to them.

What iDETECT does

  • Scan instead of typing. The app reads the printed fields from the ID using the phone camera.
  • Checks the card, not just the number. It compares the ID number, name, birthdate and validity with the registry, and returns VALID, SUSPICIOUS, SUSPENDED, INVALID or MANUAL REVIEW.
  • Reports in two taps. Staff can flag a suspicious ID to OSCA (Senior Citizens) or PDAO (PWDs) without confronting the customer.
  • Gives beneficiaries recourse. Senior Citizens and PWDs can get a notification when their ID is scanned, check their ID status, file a dispute, and report unauthorized use.

Please note

iDETECT is a student capstone prototype. It was tested with test records from consenting volunteers, not official OSCA or PDAO data, and it is not an official application of the City Government of Valenzuela or any of its offices. Results support staff judgment. They do not authorize denying a discount or confiscating an ID. Establishments should keep honoring discounts when an ID cannot be verified.

Five portals, one system

  • Business app (Android): for establishment staff.
  • Beneficiary app (Android): for Senior Citizens and PWDs. It is optional and does not affect anyone's right to the discount.
  • Web portals: for BPLO, OSCA and PDAO staff, each limited to its own role.

Built with privacy in mind

Establishments receive only a verdict, never a person's personal data. OSCA sees only Senior Citizen records, PDAO only PWD records, and BPLO only establishment functions. System actions are recorded in an audit trail. The design follows the principles of the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173).
